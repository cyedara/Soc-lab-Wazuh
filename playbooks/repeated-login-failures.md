# Playbook: Repeated Login Failures

## Objective
Investigate repeated authentication failures.

## Triage
1. Record the affected account, host, timestamp, and alert ID.
2. Review authentication logs and failure patterns.
3. Check for successful logins around the same period.
4. Distinguish expected user error from suspicious activity.

## Evidence
Preserve relevant authentication events and investigation notes.

## Escalation
Notify the authorized incident lead when suspicious activity is suspected.

## Response
Account restrictions or password resets require authorization.

## Closure
Record the findings, actions, and final disposition.
